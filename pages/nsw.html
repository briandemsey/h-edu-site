NSW Context

Understanding the landscape

New South Wales faces unique challenges in education accountability. VERA-NSW provides the verification infrastructure adapted for Australian frameworks.

LBOTE Risk Profiles

Language Background Other Than English (LBOTE) students represent a significant portion of NSW schools. VERA-NSW identifies schools where LBOTE correlates with literacy gaps, enabling targeted intervention.

FOEI & ICSEA Analysis

Family Occupation and Education Index (FOEI) and Index of Community Socio-Educational Advantage (ICSEA) provide socioeconomic context. VERA correlates these with academic outcomes.

NAPLAN Integration

National Assessment Program - Literacy and Numeracy data provides standardized benchmarks. VERA-NSW enables plain-English queries against NAPLAN results.

Data.NSW Partnership

Live integration with NSW government open data portals ensures VERA-NSW operates on current, authoritative education statistics.
