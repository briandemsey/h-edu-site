The Timeline

Indiana GPS Implementation

2025

HEA 1498 Passed

House Enrolled Act 1498 establishes the Indiana GPS framework, requiring development of "Portrait of a Graduate" with stakeholder input.

2025-26

Year Zero

Letter grades calculated and publicly released under new model. Grades are informational only — no consequences triggered. Baseline year.

2026-27

Grades Count

Full implementation. A-F grades carry legal consequences for schools. Accountability system fully operational.

The Framework

Graduate Prepared to Succeed

Indiana GPS measures success at critical K-12 milestones, with multiple pathways for students to demonstrate achievement. Not just test scores — workforce credentials, work-based learning, and college/career coursework all count.

K-3 Milestones

Foundation years focus on:

  • ILEARN test proficiency
  • Attendance markers
  • Literacy growth indicators
  • ELL goal attainment

Grades 4-8

Expanding demonstration of success:

  • Academic mastery across subjects
  • Skills development tracking
  • Additional achievement pathways

Grades 9-10

Transition to postsecondary preparation:

  • Career pathway exploration
  • Work ethic and collaboration skills
  • Academic advancement indicators

Grades 11-12

Graduation pathway completion:

  • Workforce credentials earned
  • Work-based learning experiences
  • College/career coursework
  • Regular attendance maintained
