Type 4 Detection

Identifying students who speak well but write poorly

Type 4 dyslexia candidates demonstrate strong oral communication abilities but significant challenges with written expression. VERA-TX uses the delta between TELPAS Speaking scores and Writing domain performance to flag students for further evaluation.

Oral Proficiency

TELPAS Speaking domain scores measure oral language production, fluency, and comprehension in classroom contexts.

Written Performance

TELPAS Writing domain and STAAR Reading/ELA scores capture written expression, organization, and conventions.

Delta Analysis

When the oral-written delta exceeds 8 points, the student is flagged as a Type 4 candidate requiring additional screening.

Accountability Integration

VERA-TX connects assessment data to accountability

A-F Accountability System

Texas rates schools and districts on an A-F scale across Student Achievement, School Progress, and Closing the Gaps. VERA-TX displays A-F ratings alongside Type 4 detection to show how identification gaps affect accountability.

EL Exit Criteria

Texas uses TELPAS composite and domain scores to determine English Learner exit eligibility. Students with high speaking but low writing scores may meet oral thresholds while struggling academically.

Texas Dyslexia Law

Texas requires dyslexia screening for all students. VERA-TX helps identify Type 4 candidates who may be missed by traditional screening focusing only on decoding skills.

Scale of Impact

Texas has over 1 million English Learners across 1,200+ districts. Early Type 4 identification in major districts like Houston, Dallas, and San Antonio can significantly improve statewide outcomes.
