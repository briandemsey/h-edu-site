SB 1288 Mandate

California requires LEAs to adopt AI governance policies

Senate Bill 1288 established the AI in Education Working Group to develop model policy guidance. Every California school district must adopt policies addressing:

  1. 1 Academic integrity and plagiarism in the context of AI tools
  2. 2 Acceptable and unacceptable uses of AI for pupils and educators
  3. 3 Pupil and educator data privacy and data security
  4. 4 Parent and guardian access to information pupils enter into AI systems
  5. 5 Procurement of software that ensures safety and privacy of pupils and educators
  6. 6 Effective use of AI to support teaching and learning without risk
