What We Do

Reciprocal Accountability

Accountability that flows in both directions: verifying that policy reaches students, and that student outcomes inform policy.

VERA Platform

AI-powered verification engine that connects to state education data. Plain-English queries against CAASPP, ELPAC, NAPLAN, NCEA, and equivalent assessments worldwide.

Type 4 Detection

Identifies students who speak well but write poorly - the hidden gap that current accountability systems miss entirely. The oral-written delta that predicts intervention mismatch.

LCAP Verification

Confirms whether funded interventions actually reach the students they're meant to serve. The missing link between spending and outcomes that no state has solved.
